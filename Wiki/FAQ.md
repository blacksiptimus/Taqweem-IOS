# FAQ

## How accurate are the prayer times?

Taqweem uses the aladhan API and your exact location to calculate prayer times with high accuracy.

## Why do I need location permission?

Location permission is required to calculate accurate prayer times based on your specific location.

## Can I customize which prayers notify me?

Yes! Go to Settings and enable or disable each of the five daily prayers individually.

## What happens if I don't grant notification permission?

You can still use the app, but you won't receive prayer alerts.

## Does Taqweem work offline?

The app requires an internet connection to fetch prayer times, but scheduled notifications work offline.

## How do I change my city/location?

Taqweem automatically detects your location. To manually change it, go to Settings.

## Why does the app use Islamic calendar?

Taqweem supports both Hijri and Gregorian calendars. You can switch between them in Settings.

## Is Taqweem free?

Yes! Taqweem is free to download and use.

## Where can I report issues?

Please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

## Is my data private?

Yes! Taqweem only uses your location to calculate prayer times and does not share your data with third parties.
