# Taqweem

**Your Ultimate Prayer Times Companion**

Taqweem is a beautifully crafted Islamic app that helps you never miss a prayer with accurate, timely notifications and a stunning user experience designed for modern Muslims.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Accurate Prayer Times** - Precise Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha based on your location
- **Smart Notifications** - Never miss a prayer with timely alerts and elapsed time tracking
- **Beautiful Interface** - Paper-tearing animation with multiple elegant themes
- **Dual Calendar** - Seamless Hijri and Gregorian calendar support
- **Date Search** - Jump to any date instantly
- **Customizable** - Choose which prayers trigger notifications
- **Daily Wisdom** - Stay inspired with daily quotes
- **Widget Support** - Add prayer times to your home screen
- **Watch Support** - Sync with Apple Watch

## 📱 Download Now

Get Taqweem on the App Store and keep your prayer schedule effortlessly on track.

## 🌐 Cross-Platform

Taqweem is available on iOS and Android with identical features and notification behavior.
