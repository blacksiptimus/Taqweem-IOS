Taqweem — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taqweem ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Taqweem is designed to respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

  • Location: With your permission, the app uses your device location to calculate accurate prayer times and the correct Qibla direction. Location data is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers.
  • Notifications: With your permission, the app schedules local prayer reminders and notifications. These are delivered entirely on your device.
  • Favorites & Settings: Your prayer-calendar favorites, theme choices, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Use Information

Any information we access is used solely to provide the app's core features: accurate prayer times, calendar display, Qibla direction, and reminders. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers unless you explicitly request a network feature.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may call public prayer-time or weather APIs to display religious and weather data. These requests may include your approximate location for accuracy but no personal identifiers are transmitted.

4. Data Security

Since your data remains on your device, it is protected by Apple's iOS security. We encourage you to keep your device secured.

5. Children's Privacy

Taqweem is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You can clear the app's local data or delete the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address listed in the App Store contact information.
