# Support

## Getting Help

If you have questions or need help, please:

1. Check our [FAQ](https://blacksiptimus.github.io/Taqweem-IOS/wiki/faq.html) first
2. Browse the [Wiki](https://blacksiptimus.github.io/Taqweem-IOS/wiki/)
3. Visit [Support](https://blacksiptimus.github.io/Taqweem-IOS/support/) or email [info@aqyrix.com](mailto:info@aqyrix.com)

## Reporting Issues

When reporting an issue, please include:
- Your device model and iOS / Android version
- App version
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Screenshots if applicable

You can also open a ticket on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/blacksiptimus/Taqweem-IOS/issues).

## Feature Requests

We welcome feature suggestions! Email info@aqyrix.com or open a GitHub issue with "Feature Request" in the title.
