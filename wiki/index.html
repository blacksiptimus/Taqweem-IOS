Key features

  • Accurate prayer times — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha based on your location
  • Smart notifications — Timely alerts with elapsed-time tracking
  • Beautiful interface — Paper-tearing animation with elegant themes
  • Dual calendar — Hijri and Gregorian together
  • Date search — Jump to any date instantly
  • Customizable — Choose which prayers trigger notifications
  • Daily wisdom — Stay inspired with daily quotes
  • Widget & Watch — Home screen widgets and Apple Watch support

Platforms

Taqweem is available on iOS and Android with the same core features and notification behavior.
