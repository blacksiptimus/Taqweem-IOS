# Features

## Prayer Times

Taqweem provides accurate prayer times for all five daily prayers:
- **Fajr** - Morning prayer
- **Dhuhr** - Midday prayer
- **Asr** - Afternoon prayer
- **Maghrib** - Sunset prayer
- **Isha** - Evening prayer

Times are calculated using your exact location and the aladhan API.

## Smart Notifications

Receive timely notifications before each prayer. The notification inbox shows how long each alert has been waiting with elapsed time tracking.

## Dual Calendar

Display both Islamic Hijri and Gregorian dates. The app seamlessly handles conversions between calendars.

## Date Search

Jump to any past or future date instantly by selecting from the calendar picker or entering a Hijri date.

## Customizable Notifications

Choose which prayers trigger notifications. Enable or disable each of the five daily prayers individually.

## Daily Wisdom

Stay inspired with daily wisdom reminders at a time of your choosing.

## Beautiful Themes

Choose from multiple elegant themes to match your style.

## Widget Support

Add prayer time widgets to your home screen for quick access.

## Watch Support

Sync your settings with your Apple Watch for prayer times on the go.
