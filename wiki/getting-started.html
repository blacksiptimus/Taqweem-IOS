Getting Started

Installation

  1. Open the App Store on your iOS device
  2. Search for “Taqweem”
  3. Tap Get, then Install
  4. Enter your Apple ID password if prompted

First launch

  1. Location permission — Allow Taqweem to access your location for accurate prayer times
  2. Notification permission — Enable notifications to receive prayer alerts
  3. Customize settings — Choose your preferred theme and prayer notification preferences

Quick tips

  • Enable all five daily prayers for complete notification coverage
  • Set your daily wisdom reminder time in Settings
  • Use search to jump to any date
  • Customize themes in the theme selector
