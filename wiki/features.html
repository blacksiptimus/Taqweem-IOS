Features

Prayer times

Accurate times for all five daily prayers:

  • Fajr — Morning prayer
  • Dhuhr — Midday prayer
  • Asr — Afternoon prayer
  • Maghrib — Sunset prayer
  • Isha — Evening prayer

Times are calculated from your location using the Aladhan API.

Smart notifications

Receive timely notifications before each prayer. The notification inbox shows how long each alert has been waiting with elapsed-time tracking.

Dual calendar

Display both Islamic Hijri and Gregorian dates. The app handles conversions between calendars seamlessly.

Date search

Jump to any past or future date instantly from the calendar picker or by entering a Hijri date.

Customizable notifications

Choose which prayers trigger notifications. Enable or disable each of the five daily prayers individually.

Daily wisdom

Stay inspired with daily wisdom reminders at a time you choose.

Themes, widgets & Watch

  • Multiple elegant themes
  • Home-screen prayer widgets
  • Apple Watch sync for prayer times on the go
