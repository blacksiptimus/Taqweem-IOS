FAQ

How accurate are the prayer times?

Taqweem uses the Aladhan API and your location to calculate prayer times with high accuracy.

Why do I need location permission?

Location permission is required to calculate accurate prayer times for your area, show weather, and determine Qibla direction.

Can I customize which prayers notify me?

Yes. Go to Settings and enable or disable each of the five daily prayers individually.

What if I don’t grant notification permission?

You can still use the app, but you won’t receive prayer alerts.

Does Taqweem work offline?

The app needs an internet connection to fetch prayer times. Scheduled notifications still fire offline once they are set.

How do I change my city or location?

Taqweem detects your location automatically. You can also adjust location-related options in Settings.

Why does the app use the Islamic calendar?

Taqweem supports both Hijri and Gregorian calendars so you can follow both date systems together.

Is Taqweem free?

Yes. Taqweem is free to download and use.

Where can I report issues?

See the Support page for contact options and what to include in a report.

Is my data private?

Yes. Taqweem uses your location for prayer times and related features and does not sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
